[01_title]
A small online shop gets return and refund emails. The slow part is not writing the reply. It is finding the order, and applying the policy with its date rules.

[02_edge]
I modelled the manual process at five minutes per ticket. Finding the facts and checking the draft are the steps I target. The efficiency goal is to cut staff time, and the decision goal is a reply that cites the policy and the order record.

[03_design]
From the five A's, I chose an agent, kept small. The next step depends on what a lookup returns. Tools are read only and tied to the sender's email. Code enforces the manager threshold. And a human approves every draft.
